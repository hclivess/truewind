    Reading the boat

    Telltales in the rig panel show each third of the sail: LUFF ease less or bear away, FLOW right on, STALL ease the sheet or head up. Upwind, twist is set by mainsheet tension and the jib lead; the angle by the traveler. When overpowered: backstay on, cunningham on, outhaul on, traveler down, then reef.
